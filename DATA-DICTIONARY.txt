Findings R1 replication dictionary
UTF-8 CSV files; empty cells denote missing values. IDs preserve original row
ordering. Removing names does not guarantee anonymity.

DATA-RELEASE/FACULTY.CSV —553 original faculty records
person_id: original row number+1, unique.
institution_id: numeric code of current institution in first-appearance order,
used for clustering;137 distinct values.
Rank: Assistant/Associate/Full; emeritus coded Full, no separate status flag.
PhD_Year: recorded doctoral completion year;35 missing, no baseline imputation.
Gender: ORIGINAL inferred binary F/M label; not self-identified gender. The input
preserves all original labels. Analyze.py applies the evidence correction below.
Gender_Method: historical LLM_firstpass/manual_verified/from_recruitment_verified.
Original method labels do not establish independently measured accuracy.
tier: original current-institution ranking tier1..5: ranks1-15,16-30,31-60,61-100,>100.
training_group: recorded PhD institution country, USA/Other/Unknown.
UG_training_group: undergraduate institution country, USA/China/India/Iran/Other/
Unknown; categorical model reference China. Countries are locations of awarding
institutions, not nationality, ethnicity, citizenship, or individual identity.
doctoral_training_country,undergraduate_training_country: original education
country strings, included to reproduce the audit selection.
first_hire_recorded:1 if original First_Hire nonblank,0 otherwise. Not an
independent validation of earliest tenure-track employment.
first_hire_tier: tier if the recorded first-hire institution exactly matches the
original ranking table; otherwise missing. No current-institution substitution;
nonmatching institutions are not assigned to tier5.

GENDER-AUDIT.CSV —42 selected first-pass records
person_id: joins faculty.csv.
cohort: original selection bins pre2000/2000s/2010s/2020plus/missing.
Gender: original label, also used in initial random selection.
audited_gender: F/M when explicit public pronouns support classification; blank
when unresolved. The one discordant label supplies a correction to analysis.
status: concordant(36),discordant(1),unresolved(5).
selection_phase: original19-case cohort-by-label random sample; all remaining17
known non-US doctorate first-pass cases; two additional records each from China,
India and Iran undergraduate groups (6). Audit_selection.py reproduces all IDs.
evidence_type: explicit public pronoun usage or insufficient evidence.
source_url,evidence_excerpt,checked_on: identity-matched public source, short
supporting excerpt and access date. URLs identify people. A later accessed page
is not independent evidence of January2026 appointment status.
doctoral_training_country,undergraduate_training_country: selection descriptors.
No population-weighted accuracy estimate is derived from this partly targeted audit.

KEY OUTPUTS
results/analysis_faculty.csv: original faculty input plus Gender_original,
revised Gender and profile_supported. Exactly one label differs from input Gender.
results/models.csv: non-intercept coefficients; OR,lo,hi,p and n. Decade is
(PhD_Year-2000)/10. Models are unpenalized logistic maximum likelihood. Wald
intervals are model-conditional; *_clustered uses institution sandwich uncertainty.
rank indicators reference Assistant; cohort indicators reference<=1999; tier
indicators reference1. Doctoral country references Other, undergraduate group China.
cohort_original_labels explicitly reproduces the pre-correction year association.
results/classification_sensitivity.csv: imposed adverse-error scenarios, not
estimated error rates; actual change counts supersede nominal rates. The audit
sample exclusion row removes observations. All37 audited supported labels stay
fixed during error scenarios; the5 unresolved cases remain eligible for errors.
results/missing_year_bounds.csv: separate marginal share bounds, not simultaneous
allocations or regression bounds.
results/first_hire_coverage.csv: sum=recorded values,size=total; missing=size-sum.
results/numbers.tex and other .tex tables: generated from the same results.
