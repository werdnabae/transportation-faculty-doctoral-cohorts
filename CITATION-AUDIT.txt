Citation audit completed — October 29, 2026

Result
Every cited work was identified. No fabricated journal article or mismatched DOI
was found. Three retained journal references match their canonical published
versions. One retained source is an undated, unpublished presentation manuscript,
which is explicitly identified as such rather than presented as a publication.

Checks and evidence
Saved raw Crossref records, the citation ledger, original bibliography snapshots,
and a verification script are in citation-audit/. The script checks DOI/title/
author/year/journal/volume/article identifier, use of all retained entries, matching
main/supplement labels, the unpublished source's status, and rendered n.d. citation.
Some direct publisher URLs block automated fetches; successful publisher-indexed
content, publisher-deposited Crossref metadata, and the actual published companion
PDFs provide the verification. A fetch restriction is not treated as a false DOI.
